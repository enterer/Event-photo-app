How it works

From the camera to every guest's phone, sorted for you

No tagging, no sorting thousands of photos by hand, no apps for guests to download.

  1. Upload the event

    Drag in the whole folder from your laptop. Sort photos into shared and private folders yourself, or leave them for the AI.

  2. AI sorts every photo

    Using rules you set, it shares decor, portraits of the couple and big group shots, and keeps guests' candids private. Anything it isn't sure about stays private.

  3. Guests scan and take a selfie

    A QR code on each table opens their gallery: everything shared, plus every photo they're in. Nobody else's.
