How it works

From the camera to every guest's phone, sorted for you

No tagging, no sorting thousands of photos by hand, no apps for guests to download.

  1. Upload as you shoot, or after

    Send photos from your laptop or phone during the event, or drag in the whole folder afterwards. Sort them yourself, or leave them for the AI.

  2. AI sorts every photo

    Using rules you set, it shares decor, portraits of the hosts and big group shots, and keeps guests' candids private. Anything it isn't sure about stays private.

  3. Guests scan and take a selfie

    A QR code on each table opens their gallery: everything shared, plus every photo they're in. Nobody else's.
