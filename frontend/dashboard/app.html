Sorting rules

The AI follows these for every photo the photographer leaves unsorted, and the photographer sees them as the guide on the upload page. Keep a line saying PUBLIC above the public items and PRIVATE above the private ones, one item per line starting with "-". Changes apply to new uploads; use Ask AI again on photos already sorted.

Hosts

Add the people every guest should see, such as the bride and groom, the birthday person or the guest of honour. Any unsorted photo with all of them in it is shared with everyone automatically, and the AI is told when one of them appears. Add 3 to 5 clear photos each: one person per photo, face large and in focus. Do this before the photographer uploads.
